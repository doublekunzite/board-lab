    About

    I'm Anthony 👋🏼 I've been climbing almost exclusively on the Kilter Board for a few years and I've logged over 2800 ascents. My hardest grade is V11 / 8a and I'm 5'10" tall (178cm) with a wingspan of 5'11" (180cm).

    I started climbing in Portland around 2015 but stopped when I moved to Winnipeg in 2016. I started climbing again in 2021 and have never stopped for long since then.
